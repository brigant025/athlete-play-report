How the list was built

  1. Scraped 52,535 chiropractor homepages + linked about/team/doctor pages.
  2. Keyword match: CrossFit, triathlon, triathlete, Ironman, endurance athlete, marathon, Spartan race, competitive runner, cycling, cyclist.
  3. 2,054 clinics matched at least one keyword.
  4. HubSpot filter: removed 154 companies that are current customers or have open deals.
  5. Recent-activity filter: removed 233 companies (or their contacts) with any team touchpoint in the last 60 days (since 2026-03-12).
  6. BDR-quality filter: required site-scraped doctor name + site email + phone; removed 1,110 without all three.
  7. NPI chiropractor filter: cross-referenced contacts against the NPI chiropractor registry to make sure each contact is a DC (not an LMT, PT, or other staff). Removed 206 clinics with no NPI-registered chiropractor at the location.
  8. Result: 250 clean prospects, tiered by snippet pattern.
